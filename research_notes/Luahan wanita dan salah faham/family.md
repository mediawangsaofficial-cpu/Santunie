## Which specific viral posts (with URL and date) show this misconception?

### Takeaway
Nine usable cases were found, mostly mStar write-ups of Threads luahan (2025–2026), plus one Threads post and one celebrity interview. The recurring themes are: the menantu as "kuli/bibik" in the mentua's home, the anak sulung as family ATM, the fear of "tak cium bau syurga" or being labelled derhaka for refusing, the husband's money "wajib bagi pada mak", hitting children as an outlet for exhaustion, and mom guilt.

## What exactly is the mistaken belief, and what were netizen reactions?

### Takeaway
The core mistaken beliefs are: obedience to parents is unconditional, even when it causes harm; financial support is an unlimited duty of the eldest daughter, whatever the parents' need or her own capacity; the menantu owes the mentua household service; and tiredness excuses hitting children. Netizen reactions are mixed. They usually tell anak sulung to set boundaries, tell menantu to show adab, and condemn public shaming of in-laws.

## What do credible religious, legal or psychological sources say is the correct understanding?

### Takeaway
Malaysian mufti sources say obedience to parents is wajib but not absolute: it lapses for sin and for mudarat to the child. Financial support of parents is wajib only when the parents are poor and the child is capable. It applies to sons and daughters, married or not, and should be shared among siblings. Fiqh sources say the menantu is not obliged to serve the in-laws, though ihsan is rewarded. Malaysian law criminalises child abuse with heavy penalties. Counsellors describe mom guilt as rooted in the social pressure of the "perfect mother" image.

### Cited Findings

**A. Obedience to parents is not absolute (Cases 3, 4, 5)**
- MUFTIWP Irsyad al-Hukum Siri 958, "Benarkah kita perlu mentaati ibu bapa dalam semua keadaan?": obedience is wajib but not mutlak. Following Imam al-Bulqini's criteria, it is not obligatory where the command involves sin or causes mudarat to the child. Examples given in summaries are being made to give up a livelihood or abandon studies. Derhaka is measured by local custom ('urf) and by hurting parents beyond reasonable bounds. — [MUFTIWP Irsyad al-Hukum 958](https://muftiwp.gov.my/ms/artikel/irsyad-hukum/umum/6605-irsyad-al-hukum-siri-ke-958-benarkah-kita-perlu-mentaati-ibu-bapa-dalam-semua-keadaan) (summary via search; full text not fetched)
- Perlis Mufti: parental commands that disobey Allah and His Messenger are not followed, even if the parents are upset. A child can still earn great reward by being kind to parents who failed them. — [Mufti Perlis, "Derhaka pada ibubapa yang tidak adil?"](https://muftiperlis.gov.my/index.php/minda-mufti/661-derhaka-pada-ibubapa-yang-tidak-adil)
- Counterweight: Kosmo "Tanya Mufti" says parental injustice is not a reason to be derhaka, unless the parents ask for something sinful, cruel or beyond the child's ability. — [Kosmo, 4 Mac 2022](https://www.kosmo.com.my/2022/03/04/tanya-mufti-ketidakadilan-ibu-bapa-bukan-alasan-bagi-anak-untuk-derhaka/)
- Married women: a wife's obedience to her husband takes precedence over obedience to parents in marital matters. — [MUFTIWP Irsyad Hukum 808](https://www.muftiwp.gov.my/ms/artikel/irsyad-hukum/umum/5785-irsyad-al-fatwa-siri-ke-808-mana-yang-perlu-didahulukan-ibu-dan-bapa-atau-suami). Also see Dr Zulkifli al-Bakri, #818, on parents interfering in a child's household. — [zulkiflialbakri.com](https://zulkiflialbakri.com/artikel/ibu-bapa-masuk-campur-dalam-urusan-rumah-tangga-anak/)

**B. Financial support: conditional, shared, and it applies to daughters too (Cases 3, 4, 5, and the "anak perempuan dah kahwin" misconception)**
- MUFTIWP Irsyad Hukum Siri 409/410: supporting poor parents who have no income is wajib on children, sons or daughters alike. It cites the ijma' recorded by Ibn al-Munzir. — [MUFTIWP Irsyad 409/410](https://www.muftiwp.gov.my/en/artikel/irsyad-fatwa/irsyad-fatwa-umum-cat/4150-irsyad-al-fatwa-siri-ke-409-nafkah-ibubapa-tanggungjawab-anak-lelaki-atau-anak-perempuan)
- MUFTIWP research head Dr Syed Shahridzan Syed Mohamed (Sinar Harian): in the Shafi'i school, capable children, male or female and married or not, must support poor parents. Siblings should agree on how to share the burden so one child does not carry it alone. — [Sinar Harian, "Benarkah anak perempuan tidak wajib tanggung ibu bapa?"](https://www.sinarharian.com.my/article/735598/sinar-islam/hukum/benarkah-anak-perempuan-tidak-wajib-tanggung-ibu-bapa) (date not confirmed)
- Selangor Mufti e-Musykil: one answer says there is no specific ruling on a married daughter's financial duty to her birth family, so giving is "belas ihsan dan budi bicara" according to ability. Another says the duty to poor parents falls first on sons, "tanpa menafikan tanggungjawab bagi anak perempuan". — [e-Musykil 5359](https://emusykil.muftiselangor.gov.my/index.php/site/jawapan?id=5359); [e-Musykil 6844](https://emusykil.muftiselangor.gov.my/index.php/site/jawapan?id=6844)
- Popular explainer: sons and daughters must both look after their parents after marriage. — [Mingguan Wanita](https://www.mingguanwanita.my/anak-lelaki-anak-perempuan-lepas-kahwin-sama-sama-kena-jaga-ibu-bapa/)

**C. The menantu's duty to the mentua (Cases 1, 2, 6)**
- Ustaz Mohd Masri (UtusanTV): a husband has no right to force his wife to serve his parents ("Suami tidak berhak memaksa isterinya untuk berkhidmat kepada ibu bapanya"). She may do so out of ihsan, and she may refuse to live in the same house as her in-laws. — [UtusanTV, 22 Jun 2021](https://utusantv.com/2021/06/22/hak-ibu-mertua-atas-menantu-dan-hak-isteri-enggan-serumah-dengan-mertua-ustaz-mohd-masri-beri-penjelasan-padu/)
- Selangor Mufti e-Musykil answer on mentua relations. — [e-Musykil 3086](https://emusykil.muftiselangor.gov.my/index.php/site/jawapan?id=3086) (content not read; cite only after checking)
- Classical and contemporary fatwas: Ibn Baz says the wife serves her husband, while serving the mother-in-law is makarim al-akhlaq, not a duty. Ibn Uthaimin says the wife is not obliged to serve the husband's family; that is his responsibility, and she is rewarded if she volunteers. — [binbaz.org.sa 23911](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/23911/%D9%85%D8%A7-%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC-%D9%88%D8%A8%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%87); [IslamOnline](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A9-%D8%A3%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A7/); [Dar al-Ifta Egypt](https://dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/8320/is-a-wife-obliged-to-serve-her-parents-in-law)
- A son's duty to his mother remains (Case 6): the primary responsibility for caring for parents lies with the biological son, not the menantu. — [MUFTIWP Irsyad 345, "Kasih ibu membawa ke syurga"](https://www.muftiwp.gov.my/en/artikel/irsyad-fatwa/irsyad-fatwa-umum-cat/3887-irsyad-al-fatwa-345-kasih-ibu-membawa-ke-syurga) (title only seen; content not read)

**D. Hitting children (Case 8)**
- Child Act 2001 (Akta 611) s.31(1)(a) covers ill-treatment, neglect, abandonment or exposure of a child. Since the 2016 amendment the penalty is a fine up to RM50,000, jail up to 20 years, or both. The usual defence raised in court is "mahu didik atau hukum anak yang nakal". One father was fined RM1,000 for hitting his daughter. — [Bernama Fokus](https://www.bernama.com/bm/bfokus/news.php?sosial=&id=2159496); [Akta 611 text](https://ms.wikisource.org/wiki/Akta_Kanak-Kanak_2001)
- SUHAKAM's Children's Commissioner advised parents to avoid physical punishment, especially on the child's body. — [Bernama Fokus](https://www.bernama.com/bm/bfokus/news.php?sosial=&id=2159496)
- Academic paper on child abuse by parents or guardians (UPSI). — [UPSI e-journal PERS](https://ejournal.upsi.edu.my/index.php/PERS/article/download/1709/1236/2828)

**E. Mom guilt and the "perfect mother" ideal (Cases 2, 9)**
- Counsellor Nurfilzah Emily Mohamad Khairuddin (Bernama Tinta Minda): working mothers are often haunted by guilt about not always being with their children. The comparison between working and stay-at-home mothers comes from the social pressure of the "ibu sempurna" image, and it can lead to burnout and depression. Her message is "Syurga di bawah telapak kaki ibu, bukan di atas pentas perbandingan." — [Bernama](https://bernama.com/bm/tintaminda/news.php?id=2555466); [Malaysia Gazette, 10 Mei 2026](https://malaysiagazette.com/2026/05/10/syurga-di-bawah-telapak-kaki-ibu-bukan-di-pentas-perbandingan/)

### Inferences
- For Cases 3–5, the threat "tak cium bau syurga" misreads the hadith on parents and paradise. Under Irsyad 958, a demand that harms the child, such as heavy debt or giving up marriage or studies, need not be obeyed. Kindness and adab should still be kept.
- For Case 4, the duty to fund younger siblings rests primarily on the father while he is alive and capable. That is an inference from general nafkah fiqh; no Malaysian source was found in this session.
- For Case 2, mentua moving in does not create a fiqh obligation of service on the wife. It is the husband's responsibility to arrange care and the household. The UtusanTV and Ibn Uthaimin positions support this.

### Gaps
- No MUFTIWP Irsyad specifically on "isteri wajib berkhidmat kepada mentua" was found. A secondhand claim cites "Irsyad Fatwa No. 579" but could not be verified, so do not use it.
- No Malaysian fatwa source was found on wali adhal (a wali unreasonably refusing marriage). Islamic family law in Malaysia lets a woman apply to the Syariah court for wali hakim, but no source was retrieved in this session, so verify against the relevant state Enakmen Undang-Undang Keluarga Islam before citing.
- No LPPKN source was retrieved, and no Malaysian source on the "ringan" hitting for solat at age 10 hadith, which is relevant to the hitting-children debate.
- No authoritative source was found on estrangement from abusive parents ("putus hubungan").

## Is the correction settled or contested?

### Takeaway
Some corrections are clearly settled and some are contested. Settled: conditional obedience, daughters' duty to poor parents, and the illegality of abuse. Contested: whether, and how much, a married daughter must give when parents are not poor; how far a menantu must help in the mentua's home (adab vs wajib); "low-contact" with toxic parents; and the line between light discipline and abuse.

### Cited Findings
- Settled: obedience is wajib but not mutlak, and lapses for sin or mudarat. — [MUFTIWP 958](https://muftiwp.gov.my/ms/artikel/irsyad-hukum/umum/6605-irsyad-al-hukum-siri-ke-958-benarkah-kita-perlu-mentaati-ibu-bapa-dalam-semua-keadaan); [Mufti Perlis](https://muftiperlis.gov.my/index.php/minda-mufti/661-derhaka-pada-ibubapa-yang-tidak-adil)
- Settled: poor parents must be supported by capable children of either sex, with ijma' cited. — [MUFTIWP 409/410](https://www.muftiwp.gov.my/en/artikel/irsyad-fatwa/irsyad-fatwa-umum-cat/4150-irsyad-al-fatwa-siri-ke-409-nafkah-ibubapa-tanggungjawab-anak-lelaki-atau-anak-perempuan)
- Contested: whether sons take priority, and whether a married daughter's giving is wajib or only ihsan when parents are not destitute. MUFTIWP/Sinar Harian lean towards "same duty", while Selangor e-Musykil leans towards "sons first; daughters by ihsan/ability". — [Sinar Harian](https://www.sinarharian.com.my/article/735598/sinar-islam/hukum/benarkah-anak-perempuan-tidak-wajib-tanggung-ibu-bapa); [e-Musykil 6844](https://emusykil.muftiselangor.gov.my/index.php/site/jawapan?id=6844); [e-Musykil 5359](https://emusykil.muftiselangor.gov.my/index.php/site/jawapan?id=5359)
- Contested (fiqh vs 'urf): the wife's service duties. Ibn Baz holds that serving the husband is obligatory, while other schools treat it as non-obligatory. Serving the in-laws is widely held not to be wajib, but Malay 'urf strongly expects help, and netizens enforce that expectation. — [binbaz.org.sa 8738](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/8738/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A7); [UtusanTV](https://utusantv.com/2021/06/22/hak-ibu-mertua-atas-menantu-dan-hak-isteri-enggan-serumah-dengan-mertua-ustaz-mohd-masri-beri-penjelasan-padu/); [mStar Case 1](https://www.mstar.com.my/lokal/viral/2025/05/23/mentang-mentang-mentua-baik-menantu-jadikan-kuli-wanita-bidas-kaum-sendiri-goyang-kaki-balik-kampung-suami)
- Contested: whether "unfair parents" justify any distance. The Kosmo Tanya Mufti column stresses that injustice is no excuse for derhaka, while Perlis stresses not obeying sinful commands. — [Kosmo](https://www.kosmo.com.my/2022/03/04/tanya-mufti-ketidakadilan-ibu-bapa-bukan-alasan-bagi-anak-untuk-derhaka/)
- Legal grey area: the sources do not define where "reasonable" physical discipline ends under s.31 Child Act. — [Bernama Fokus](https://www.bernama.com/bm/bfokus/news.php?sosial=&id=2159496)

### Inferences
- The safest framing for content is "taat ≠ ikut semua; tegas dengan adab". Kindness and ziarah stay obligatory, while harmful demands, such as loans or giving up marriage or studies, can be declined respectfully.
- The menantu topic should be framed as "tidak wajib secara fiqh, tetapi ihsan dan adab digalakkan; tanggungjawab utama pada anak lelaki". That avoids both extremes.
